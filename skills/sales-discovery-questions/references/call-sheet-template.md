# Discovery call sheet - fill-in template

One page. The rep reads it 20 minutes before the call; it must survive being skimmed.

```
DISCOVERY CALL SHEET
Prospect   : <persona, title, company>          Call: <type>, <length>
Origin     : <inbound / outbound + what they responded to>
Known      : <2-4 bullets from prior touches - never re-ask these>
Next step  : <the success outcome this call closes toward, with a date>

HYPOTHESES (test, don't confirm)
H1: <suspected pain - ranked first>
H2: <alternate>
(H3: <fallback>)

FRAME
"<one-line upfront agenda - purpose, time, decide-together outcome>"

QUESTION PATH
[Situation - max 2, only what research couldn't answer]
Q: <question>
   listen for: <signal>            exit: current process in their words

[Pain]
Q: <primary, tied to H1>
   ladder: <rung 1> / <rung 2> / <rung 3>
   listen for: <a recent, specific example>   exit: named problem + example

[Root cause]
Q: <primary>
   ladder: <prior attempts> / <did it work>
   exit: cause ≠ symptom; attempts known

[Impact - need a figure, ≥2 of technical/business/personal]
Q: <business-impact question>
Q: <technical- or personal-impact question>
   listen for: their number, who else is touched
   exit: at least one quantified figure, in their words

[Urgency]
Q: <why-now question>
Q: <cost-of-inaction question>
   exit: dated/triggered reason to act - or its honest absence, noted

[Decision context - complex B2B only]
Q: <process question>   Q: <other-voices question>
   exit: path to a decision sketched (feeds the scorecard - no score here)

CLOSE
Summary: "So if I've got it - <pain>, because <cause>, costing <figure>..."
Ask    : "<specific next step> on <date>?"

BRANCHES
- No pain after H1+H2 → <fallback question>; do not manufacture pain
- Impact volunteered early → bank it, loop back for root cause
- Executive tone → halve the questions, lead with sharpest hypothesis + POV
- Price/demo asked mid-spine → brief answer, trade back one stage

DO NOT ASK
- Timeline or budget before impact
- "Would you buy/use...?" hypotheticals
- <deal-specific leading question to avoid>

AFTER THE CALL (fill in)
Stages unmet: <which exit conditions failed - these are the next-touch gaps>
```
